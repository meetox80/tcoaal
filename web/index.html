Details

    

  


  

    

      
Put it on your PSP

      

        
        
      

      
    
        
  1. Unzip it into the root of your memory stick, so you end up with PSP/GAME/TCOAAL/EBOOT.PBP.
    2. 
        
  2. On the PSP, open Game, then Memory Stick. In PPSSPP, open the EBOOT directly.
    3. 
        
  3. Saves go into PSP/GAME/TCOAAL/saves and are kept when you update.
    4. 
      

      
Controls: d-pad to move, cross to interact, circle to cancel, triangle for the menu, square to run, R to fast-forward dialogue.

    

    

      
Install it on your PS Vita

      

        
        
      

      
    
        
  1. Unzip the data folder into ux0: on your Vita, for example over VitaShell's FTP or USB connection, so you end up with ux0:data/TCOAAL/tcoaal.pak.
    2. 
        
  2. Copy TCOAAL.vpk to the Vita and install it with VitaShell. The game shows up on the home screen.
    3. 
        
  3. Saves go into ux0:data/TCOAAL/saves and are kept when you update.
    4. 
      

      
Controls: d-pad or left stick to move, cross to interact, circle to cancel, triangle or start for the menu, hold square to run, hold R to fast-forward dialogue.

    

    

      
Put it on your SD card

      

        
      

      
    
        
  1. Unzip it into the root of your SD card or USB drive, so you end up with apps/tcoaal/boot.dol and apps/tcoaal/tcoaal.pak.
    2. 
        
  2. Start it from the Homebrew Channel.
    3. 
        
  3. Saves go into apps/tcoaal/saves and are kept when you update.
    4. 
      

      
Controls: hold the Wii Remote sideways. D-pad to move, 2 to interact, 1 to cancel, + for the menu, hold B to run, hold A to fast-forward dialogue. On a Classic Controller or GameCube controller: d-pad or stick to move, A to interact, B to cancel, X for the menu, hold Y to run, hold R to fast-forward dialogue.

    

    

      
Put it on your SD card

      

        
      

      
    
        
  1. Unzip it into the root of your SD card, so you end up with wiiu/apps/tcoaal/tcoaal.wuhb and tcoaal.pak next to it.
    2. 
        
  2. Start it from the Wii U Menu.
    3. 
        
  3. Saves go into wiiu/apps/tcoaal/saves and are kept when you update.
    4. 
      

      
Controls: d-pad or left stick to move, A to interact, B to cancel, X or + for the menu, hold Y to run, hold R to fast-forward dialogue.

    

    

      
Put it on a USB drive

      

        
      

      
    
        
  1. Unzip it into the root of a FAT32 USB drive, so you end up with xenon.elf and tcoaal/tcoaal.pak.
    2. 
        
  2. Plug the drive into the Xbox 360 and boot XeLL. It loads xenon.elf from the drive and the game starts.
    3. 
        
  3. Saves go into tcoaal/saves on the drive and are kept when you update.
    4. 
      

      
Controls: d-pad or left stick to move, A to interact, B to cancel, Y or start for the menu, hold X to run, hold RB or RT to fast-forward dialogue.

    

    

      
Play it

      

        
        
      

      
    
        
  1. In Azahar, open File, then Load File, and pick tcoaal.3dsx.
    2. 
        
  2. On a 3DS, unzip the SD card folder into the root of your SD card, so you end up with 3ds/tcoaal/tcoaal.3dsx, then start it from the Homebrew Launcher.
    3. 
        
  3. Sound needs 3ds/dspfirm.cdc on the SD card, which DSP1 creates during the usual custom firmware setup. Saves go into 3ds/tcoaal/saves and are kept when you update.
    4. 
      

      
Controls: d-pad or Circle Pad to move, A to interact, B to cancel, X for the menu, Y to run, R to fast-forward dialogue. The touchscreen has buttons to toggle running and fast-forward, open the menu and save.

    

    

      
Play it on your PS2

      

        
        
      

      
    
        
  1. Open PS2 Loader: put TCOAAL.iso in the DVD folder of your USB drive, hard drive or network share, then start it from the game list.
    2. 
        
  2. PCSX2: open TCOAAL.iso with File, then Start File.
    3. 
        
  3. uLaunchELF: unzip the USB folder onto your USB drive and start TCOAAL.ELF. Keep TCOAAL.PAK next to it.
    4. 
        
  4. Saves go to the memory card in slot 1, in a folder called TCOAAL, or slot 2 if slot 1 is empty.
    5. 
      

      
Controls: d-pad to move, cross to interact, circle to cancel, triangle for the menu, hold square to run, hold R1 to fast-forward dialogue.

    

    

      
Install it on your PS3

      

        
      

      
    
        
  1. Real PS3: copy TCOAAL.pkg to the root of a USB drive, then install it with Package Manager, Install Package Files. The game shows up under Game on the XMB.
    2. 
        
  2. RPCS3: install TCOAAL.pkg with File, Install Packages, then start it from the game list.
    3. 
        
  3. Saves are kept on the hard drive with the game and stay when you update.
    4. 
      

      
Controls: d-pad to move, cross to interact, circle to cancel, triangle or start for the menu, hold square to run, hold R1 or R2 to fast-forward dialogue.

    

  


  

    
Put it on the HOME menu (optional)

    
Turn your build into tcoaal.cia, which installs like a regular game, with its own icon on the HOME menu. This takes a few seconds and doesn't touch your game files again.

    3. 
      
  3. The installed game and tcoaal.3dsx share the same saves in 3ds/tcoaal/saves.
